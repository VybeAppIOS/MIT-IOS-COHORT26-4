# Prompt: find a new app name (copy everything below the line into any AI agent)

Use this with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any agent that can browse the web. Give the same
prompt to two or three different tools and compare their shortlists. A human (you) must
re-check every final name yourself on the official sites before anything is bought or
published.

---

You are a brand-naming researcher with web access. Help four college students in Pune,
India, find a name for their iOS app. Plan first, then research. Do not write any code.

## The app
- An iPhone app for Gen Z college students (18 to 23) in Indian cities, starting in
  Kothrud, Pune.
- People discover real local places (cafés, street food, parks, game zones, treks) and
  do multi-stop outings called **Hangouts** with friends: save a Hangout, remix it, run it
  live on a map, then post what they did. There are mutual friends, a feed, group chat.
- Tone: fun, reliable, mischievous friend with light meme-style humour ("Find your Vybe",
  "One more spot?"). Low budget, high spontaneity, friend groups.
- Words already used in the app: Hangout, Spot, Remix, Picks.
- Working name "Vybe" is **not usable**: two going-out apps called "Vybe: Go Out Smarter"
  and "Vybe - The Going Out App" are already on the App Store, and a US "VYBE" software
  trademark appears registered. Do not suggest Vybe, Vibe, Vybes, Vybz or close spellings.

## Hard rules (do not break these)
1. **Never suggest a name that is already taken.** Before putting any name on your
   shortlist you must check, with evidence (a link or a quoted search result) for each:
   a. **Apple App Store**: search the exact name and close variants. If any app, in any
      country, uses the same or a confusingly similar name, reject it. (App Store names
      must be 2 to 30 characters and unique.)
   b. **Google Play**: same check.
   c. **Trademarks**: search the official **IP India** public search
      (tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in) and **USPTO** (tmsearch.uspto.gov) for the name in
      software and social-networking classes (Class 9, 42, 45). If you cannot reach the
      official site, say so clearly and mark the name "trademark NOT verified".
   d. **Domains**: check whether the .com, .in and .app (or .co) domains are free.
   e. **Social handles**: check Instagram, X and YouTube for the exact handle.
2. If you cannot verify a check, write **"UNVERIFIED"** next to it. Never guess and never
   say a name is "available" without evidence.
3. Reject names that are hard for an Indian Gen Z user to spell or say, that sound like an
   existing big brand, or that mean something rude, sad or wrong in **Hindi, Marathi or
   Hinglish** slang. Say which languages you checked.
4. The name must work as an App Store name of **30 characters or fewer**, in Latin letters,
   with a short subtitle (up to 30 characters) like "Hangouts with your people".
5. Do not use the word "college" or any college's name (the app is for many campuses).
6. The name should not need the word "app" and should not copy the vocabulary of rivals
   (District, Zomato, Swiggy, magicpin, Partiful, Beli, Snapchat, Instagram).

## What to produce
1. A short list of **naming directions** (for example: playful verbs, Hinglish words,
   place metaphors, short invented words) with a one-line reason each.
2. **20 candidate names**, then eliminate down to a **shortlist of 5 to 8** that pass every
   hard rule.
3. For each shortlisted name a table with: name; why it fits; how to say it; meaning in
   Hindi/Marathi/Hinglish; App Store check (with link); Google Play check (with link);
   IP India check (result); USPTO check (result); .com / .in / .app domain status;
   Instagram / X / YouTube handle status; a 30-character subtitle idea; risks.
4. A list of names you **rejected and why** (taken, similar to something, bad meaning).
5. Your recommendation, and 3 questions the team should answer before choosing.

Be strict and honest. A shorter list of names that are really free is better than a long
list that is not.
